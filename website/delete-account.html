Delete Your Satguru Panth Account

Permanently remove your account and all associated data.

Satguru Panth (developed by Amish Mishra; package com.satgurupanth.satguruapp) is a mobile app for reading sacred and devotional texts. You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app, or by emailing us if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Satguru Panth app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (rightmost icon in the bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Continue on the warning, then Delete forever on the final confirmation.

Your account and all associated data are deleted from our servers immediately after the second confirmation. The app signs you out automatically.

Option 2 — Delete by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the same Google account you used to sign in:

amish.silent.killer.888@gmail.com

Use the subject line "Delete my Satguru Panth account". We process requests within 7 business days and email you a confirmation once the data has been deleted.

What data is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture obtained from Google)
  • Your list of liked books
  • Any in-app preferences tied to your account

What data is kept

Aggregate, non-identifying statistics — for example "how many times a book has been viewed across all users" — are retained because they are no longer linked to any individual after deletion. No personal data remains.

Retention period

Personal data is deleted immediately. Server logs that may have captured incidental request metadata are purged within 30 days by automatic log rotation. Backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.

Can't access the app or your Google account? Email us anyway from any address you can prove ownership of, and we'll help verify your identity before deletion.
