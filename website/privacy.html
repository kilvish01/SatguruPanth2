Privacy Policy

Satguru Panth · Last updated: 12 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Satguru Panth mobile application (package com.satgurupanth.satguruapp, developed by Amish Mishra; "we", "our", "us") collects, uses and stores your information. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

The app collects the following data only when you sign in:

  • Name — obtained from your Google account at sign-in.
  • Email address — obtained from your Google account at sign-in (used as your unique identifier).
  • Profile picture URL — obtained from your Google account, used to display your avatar in the app.
  • In-app interactions — the books you like and your reading activity, stored so we can show them back to you across devices.

We do not collect: phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, photos, files, microphone audio, camera, or any health/financial data.

How we use this information

  • To identify your account and let you sign back in on other devices.
  • To remember which books you have liked, so they appear in your library.
  • To show aggregate counts (e.g. "most liked books") to all users; these counts are not linked back to you.

How we authenticate you

Sign-in is performed exclusively through Google Sign-In. We never see or store your Google password. After a successful sign-in we receive a Google-issued identity token, verify it server-side and issue our own short-lived authentication token to your device.

Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS) DynamoDB in the us-east-1 (United States) region. All traffic between the app and our backend uses HTTPS (TLS). We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Third-party services we rely on

  • Google Sign-In — solely for authentication. Google's own privacy policy governs the data they hold about you: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — for backend hosting and database storage. AWS's privacy notice: aws.amazon.com/privacy.

How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all personal data is removed immediately from our database; server logs containing incidental metadata are purged within 30 days by automatic log rotation; backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.

How to delete your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time directly from the app: Profile → Delete account. Detailed steps, including how to request deletion by email if you no longer have the app installed, are documented here: Delete my account.

Children's privacy

Satguru Panth is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us using the email below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle your data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Please review periodically.
